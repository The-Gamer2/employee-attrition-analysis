# Employee Attrition Analysis

This project is part of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Science Internship Programme – Week 1**.

## Project Overview
The goal of this project is to analyze employee attrition at ABC Manufacturing Ltd using the IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.  
The analysis focuses on understanding the business problem, inspecting the dataset, exploring workforce patterns, and generating initial insights that can help HR make better decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Understand the business problem behind employee attrition.
- Inspect and validate the dataset.
- Perform initial exploratory data analysis.
- Identify possible factors contributing to attrition.
- Generate business insights to support HR decisions.

## Dataset
- **Dataset Name:** IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Rows:** 1,470
- **Columns:** 35

## Key Analysis Steps
- Data loading and inspection.
- Data quality checks.
- Exploratory data analysis.
- Visualization of attrition patterns.
- Interpretation of charts and business insights.

## Visualizations Included
- Bar charts
- Histograms
- Boxplots
- Pie chart

## Main Findings
- Attrition is influenced by multiple factors such as overtime, income, age, department, and job role.
- The dataset is clean, with no missing values and no duplicate rows.
- The attrition target variable is imbalanced, with more employees staying than leaving.
